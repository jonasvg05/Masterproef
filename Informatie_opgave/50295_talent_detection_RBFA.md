## Problem

Soccer talent detection and development has become an extremely important issue for most clubs all over the world, i.e., it has become a true necessity in today'soccer.
More and more data is also becoming available from players at all levels. RBFA (the Royal Belgian Football Association) is interested to investigate what we can do with al this data.

What can we learn from selections and play times of young players in the national youth teams/local elite teams?

Can AI/machine learning help in detecting talents from data collected at talentdays?
If so, there can be worked with them more efficiently at a younger age.

## Goal

With the help of AI/Machine learning this thesis would aim to show which parameters or combination of parameters can contribute to an early identification of future top talent potential Red Devils.

We hope to find these important parameters in:

1. the datasets of selections of young players for the national youth teams
2. their play times in these teams
3. data collected from collaboration between RBFA and SciSport
4. focus on event data and tracking data
